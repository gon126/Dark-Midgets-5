⚔ Tutorial Guide ⚔

🎮 Movement & Controls

  • Use WASD to move your character
  • Press Space to shoot at enemies
  • Press P or ESC to pause the game

⚔ Choosing Your Class

  • Ranger - Balanced, good for beginners
  • Gunner - Fast firing, lower damage
  • Sniper - High damage, slow cooldown
  • Scout - Fastest movement speed
  • Berserker - Massive damage, very slow attacks
  • And many more classes to discover...

💀 Survival Tips

  • ⚠ Keep moving! Standing still makes you an easy target
  • Collect XP orbs to level up and gain upgrades
  • Every 5 rounds you get a rest period to choose upgrades
  • Watch out for boss rounds (round 5, 10, 15...)
  • Use obstacles and barrels for cover
