## 🧙 Character Classes (14 Total)

### Easy to Learn
- **Ranger** (Recommended for beginners) - Balanced stats
- **Acolyte** - Balanced + health regeneration

### High Damage
- **Sniper** - Massive damage, slow fire rate
- **Gunner** - Fast fire rate, lower damage
- **Revenant** - High damage but very fragile

### Tank/Defense
- **Brawler** - High HP and damage
- **Templar** - Highest health pool
- **Paladin** - Best regeneration rate

### Speed/Agility
- **Scout** - Fastest movement
- **Engineer** - Fastest fire rate

### Special
- **Berserker** - Extreme damage (experimental)
- **Warlock** - Life steal and damage
- **Summoner** - High HP, fast firing
- **Alchemist** - Area damage strategy

## 💎 Progression System

Earn **Soul currency** after each run by surviving longer. Spend it to unlock permanent upgrades:
- **HP Buff** - Increase maximum health (+5% per level)
- **Speed Buff** - Increase movement speed (+5% per level)

Your upgrades carry over to every future run!

## 💡 Tips for Success

### First Time Playing?
1. Start with **Ranger** class
2. Choose **Easy** difficulty
3. Keep moving constantly
4. Collect XP orbs to level up
5. Learn enemy patterns

### Advanced Strategies
- Use corners and obstacles for cover
- Choose upgrades that match your playstyle
- Rotate through different classes
- Play multiple runs to earn Soul upgrades
- Try Hardcore mode once you're confident
